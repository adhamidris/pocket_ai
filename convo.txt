  Based on my deep investigation of the codebase, here's my comprehensive analysis:

  What's Happening

  When you ask "what are the fees for Withdraw Bills for Collection?":

  1. Query → Embedding: The query gets embedded as a 384-dim vector using paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2
  2. Vector Search: Azure/Postgres finds chunks with similar embeddings
  3. Hybrid Search: BM25 lexical search also runs on tokens like "fees", "withdraw", "collection"
  4. Ranking: Multi-factor scoring: vector(1.0) + lexical(0.8) + alias(1.2) + entity(0.4) + recency(0.25)
  5. LLM Selection: The LLM receives ranked snippets and answers from top results

  The Problem: Steps 2-4 don't have enough document context discrimination.

  ---
  Root Causes

  1. Semantic Embedding Collision (Primary)

  All your banking documents share similar vocabulary:
  - "Trade Bills EN" has: fees, bills, collection, withdraw
  - "International Withdrawals" has: fees, withdrawal, ATM, cash
  - "Smart Wallet" has: fees, loading, wallet

  The 384-dim MiniLM model encodes these as semantically similar because they're all "banking fee" concepts. Vector distance between:
  - "Withdraw Bills for Collection" ↔ "Cash withdrawal from ATMs" = LOW (similar semantics)
  - But they're from completely different business operations

  Evidence: ai_orchestrator.py:3591 - vector score = 1.0 - cosine_distance, but all fee-related chunks have similar distances.
